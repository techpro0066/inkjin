# Changes 14 Nov 2026: invitations without a split

## Why

An artist and a studio could already be linked without a revenue split when the studio is on Bookpay (the studio invites with "Workstation rent", or the artist sends a join request with "Artist - Direct payment"). The gap was a studio that is **not on Bookpay**: the artist could invite it only together with a revenue split. An artist who rents a workstation, or a guest artist paid directly, had no way to bring the studio in, so the studio could never list them on its page.

## Rules

1. The invitation is separate from the money. With direct payment the invite is optional. With a revenue split it is required, as before.
2. A no split invite never needs Stripe on the studio side.
3. The artist keeps working and getting paid while the invite waits. Nothing is blocked.
4. When the studio accepts, the link gets `arrangement = rent` (resident) or the guest spot keeps `payout_mode = direct`. The artist then shows in the studio's Artists or Guest artists list and can be shown on the studio page.
5. Cancelling the invite changes nothing about how the artist is paid.

## Data

- `studio_invites`: `arrangement = rent` with no proposed split is now allowed for invites sent by an artist (it was already allowed for invites sent by a studio).
- `guest_spots`: add `invite_studio` (boolean, default false) for a direct payment guest spot at a studio not on Bookpay.

## Decided: a studio sees nothing for an artist with no split

Decided 14 Nov 2026. It replaces the earlier rule "You still see their bookings at your studio" (`06-STUDIO-ONBOARDING.md`, step 4).

**The rule.** An artist with no split at a studio (workstation rent, or a guest spot on direct payment) is listed by the studio, and that is all the studio gets.

| The studio | With a revenue split | With no split |
|---|---|---|
| Lists the artist in Artists or Guest artists | Yes | Yes |
| Shows the artist on its studio page | Yes | Yes |
| Sees the artist's bookings and requests | Yes | No |
| Sees the artist in its calendar | Yes | No, not even as Busy |
| Counts the artist's bookings in its numbers | Yes | No |
| Makes a booking or payment link for the artist (New booking) | Yes | No, the artist isn't in the list |
| Assigns a studio request to the artist | Yes | No |
| Its consent form is used for the artist's sessions | Yes (studio rule) | No, the artist's own form |
| Can remove the artist | Yes | Yes |

Clients still reach the artist from the studio page: the artist's card opens the artist's own booking form.

**Data and checks**

- Every studio query for bookings, requests, payment links and calendar events filters on `artist_studios.arrangement = 'split'` (and for guest spots on `guest_spots.payout_mode = 'split'`). Check this on the server, not only in the page.
- A booking keeps the setup it was made with. If an artist moves from a split to rent, the studio still sees the bookings made during the split, and none made after.
- When an artist with no split leaves or is removed, there is nothing to reassign.
